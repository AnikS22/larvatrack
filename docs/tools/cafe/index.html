Feeding (CAFE assay)

Measures how much flies drank from each capillary, from one photo of the tubes next to a ruler. Length of liquid gone × volume per millimetre = microlitres eaten.

How it works
  1. Upload a photo of the capillaries lying next to a ruler.
  2. Scale: click two marks on the ruler and type the distance between them.
  3. Tubes: for each capillary, click its start mark, then the food level after feeding. Give it a group and food.
  4. Wrong click? Press Undo, or delete the tube in the table and measure it again.
