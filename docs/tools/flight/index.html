Flight (fly paper)

Flies dropped down a tube catch the sticky paper as they recover flight. Strong fliers stick high; weak ones fall to the water. This counts flies per interval, from Interval 1 (water) up to the top of the paper.

How it works
  1. Upload a photo of one fly-paper sheet and say which side of the photo is the top of the paper.
  2. Lines: click on each red dividing line.
  3. Flies: click every real fly. Click a circle again to remove it. Printed flies don't count.
  4. Type how many flies were in the water, name the genotype, and save the sheet. Repeat for each sheet.
